Welcome to Phakama Eastern Cape

Building Strong Communities.
Strengthening the Eastern Cape.

Phakama Eastern Cape is a people-powered civic movement working alongside communities to build a stronger, more prosperous and more accountable Eastern Cape.

Established in September 2025, Phakama Eastern Cape brings together citizens, traditional leaders, government, educational institutions, business, faith-based organisations and civil society to identify challenges, build partnerships and implement practical solutions.

Working across the seven regions of the Eastern Cape, we promote ethical leadership, active citizenship, accountable governance and sustainable community development through collaboration rather than confrontation.

Although communities may mandate Phakama Eastern Cape to participate in democratic processes where appropriate, our identity remains that of a civic movement committed to serving communities before, during and beyond election cycles.

Together, we are Rescuing and Reviving the Eastern Cape.

Why Phakama Eastern Cape Is Different

Community-Led.
Partnership-Driven.
Solutions-Focused.

Many organisations speak for communities. Phakama Eastern Cape works with communities. Our approach is built on partnership, accountability and practical action that creates lasting impact.

01

Community First

Everything begins by listening. Our programmes are shaped by the priorities, aspirations and needs identified by communities themselves.

02

Partnership Driven

No single organisation can transform the Eastern Cape. We bring together communities, government, business, educational institutions, traditional leadership and civil society to develop shared solutions.

03

Solutions Focused

We move beyond identifying problems. Our work centres on practical implementation, collaboration and sustainable outcomes that improve people's lives.

04

Province-Wide Reach

Through growing regional structures, volunteers and partnerships, Phakama Eastern Cape is steadily expanding its footprint across all seven regions.

05

Professionally Governed

Our governance combines community leadership with professional expertise, sound governance systems and ethical accountability.

06

Built to Last

Phakama Eastern Cape is building a sustainable civic institution that serves communities every day - not only during election periods.
