Welcome to Phakama

Who We
Are.

Learn who we are, what we stand for and how we are working with communities across the Eastern Cape to strengthen accountability, promote development and build a better future for all.

Although Phakama will participate in democratic processes where communities mandate us to do so, our identity remains that of a civic movement committed to serving communities beyond election cycles.
