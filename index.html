Welcome to Phakama

Who We Are.

Learn who we are, what we stand for and how we are working with communities across the Eastern Cape to strengthen accountability, promote development and build a better future for all.

Although Phakama will participate in democratic processes where communities mandate us to do so, our identity remains that of a civic movement committed to serving communities beyond election cycles.

Our Approach

How We Work.

Lasting change happens when communities, government, traditional leaders, business and civil society work together around a shared vision for the future.

01

Community First

We listen before we act, ensuring that our work is informed by the real needs and aspirations of communities.

02

Partnership over Confrontation

We believe respectful engagement, collaboration and evidence-based advocacy produce more sustainable outcomes than conflict alone.

03

From Ideas to Action

We strive to move beyond identifying problems by building partnerships, supporting implementation and contributing to long-term solutions.

Current Priorities

What We're Focused On.

🎓

Community Career Exhibition & Rural Development Dialogue

Preparing young people for brighter futures through education, career guidance and rural development dialogue in Mbashe Local Municipality.

🏦

Phakama Cooperative Financial Institution (CFI)

Building community-owned financial solutions that promote savings, investment and economic empowerment.

🇿🇦

Community Safety & Rule of Law

Promoting lawful governance, effective law enforcement and safer communities through constructive civic engagement and advocacy.

🗳️

Local Government Election Readiness

Preparing to strengthen local democracy by promoting ethical leadership, active citizenship and community-centred representation in the 2026 Local Government Elections.

🤝

Strategic Partnerships & Organisational Growth

Building strong partnerships, strengthening organisational capacity and expanding Phakama's provincial footprint to increase sustainable community impact.
